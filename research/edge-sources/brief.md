# Brief: Real Market Edge Sources for Small Capital ($300-500)

## Question
What are real, academically or practically proven sources of trading edge that could work for a trader with $300-500 capital on MT5 (forex/gold) or crypto exchanges?

## Scope
- IN: Academic papers, known profitable strategies, microstructure edge, order flow, volatility, execution, regime-based
- IN: Both MT5 instruments (XAUUSD, forex) and crypto (BingX futures)
- IN: Strategies actually used by profitable funds/traders
- OUT: Simple indicator combinations (EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger)
- OUT: Grid, Martingale, DCA, averaging
- OUT: Theoretical-only approaches requiring institutional infrastructure

## Constraints
- Capital: $300-500
- Platforms: MT5 (RoboForex), BingX futures
- Instruments: XAUUSD, BTC, ETH, SOL, major forex
- Must be testable in 2-3 days without new architecture

## Depth: standard
## Date: 2026-07-21

## Angles
1. **Volatility Risk Premium (VRP)** — selling implied vol when elevated, well-documented
2. **Funding Rate Arbitrage / Mean Reversion in Crypto** — unique to crypto markets
3. **Cross-Asset Momentum / Carry** — academic research on momentum and carry factors
4. **Market Microstructure / Order Flow** — how HFT and prop firms extract edge
5. **Execution Quality / Slippage Alpha** — edge from better execution
6. **Volatility Regime / Adaptive Strategies** — how regime detection adds value
7. **Seasonal / Calendar Effects** — turn-of-month, options expiry, session-based
8. **MVP (Minimum Variance Portfolio) / Risk Parity** — systematic risk premia
9. **Sentiment / Positioning Data** — COT, funding, open interest signals
10. **Mean Reversion at Ultra-Short Horizons** — scalping with microstructure edge
