## Failure Mode
**High winrate / negative expectancy (payoff asymmetry).**
- Small TP (0.3%) consumed by costs (commission + spread + slippage)
- Fixed SL (0.5%) broken by volatility regime changes (ATR >> SL)
- No market regime filter — counter-trend logic catches falling knives in strong trends

## Reusable Knowledge
1. TP must be > costs × safety margin (0.3% is insufficient at 0.06% commission)
2. SL must be ATR-adaptive, not fixed — fixed SL either too tight (high vol) or too loose (low vol)
3. Counter-trend strategies require regime detection to avoid trading into strong momentum
